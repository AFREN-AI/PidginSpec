# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive experience for everyone who participates in this project.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include respectful language, constructive feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, publishing another person's private information, or conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report conduct concerns through the repository's private contact options.

This code of conduct is adapted from Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
